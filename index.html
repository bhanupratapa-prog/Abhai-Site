The Challenge

Every summer, India's forests burn faster than we can watch them.

From the pine slopes of Uttarakhand and Himachal to the sal forests of Odisha and the Eastern Ghats, lakhs of hectares are lost each fire season. Watchtowers see only fragments. Satellites report hours late. By the time smoke is visible from the ground, the window for early attack has already closed.

Detect in under 5 minutes

The first minutes decide everything. Abhai's AI identifies fire and smoke signatures the moment they appear — long before human eyes can.

Respond while it's still small

Enable initial attack within 10 minutes, while the fire is under one hectare — the difference between a routine callout and a week-long disaster.

Watch everything, always

Continuous 24×7 surveillance of vast forest divisions — through night, haze, and terrain no watchtower or patrol jeep can cover.

Proven, Not Promised

The intelligence inside Abhai is already flying.

This is not a lab experiment. The AI models at the heart of Abhai are field-deployed overseas, operating live with professional fire and emergency services in Europe — patrolling real forests, detecting real fires, and triggering real responses, season after season.

Abhai holds the exclusive rights to bring this proven technology home — re-engineered for Indian terrain, Indian weather, and Indian forest departments.
