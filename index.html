The Challenge

Every summer, India's forests burn faster than we can watch them.

From the pine slopes of Uttarakhand and Himachal to the sal forests of Odisha and the Eastern Ghats, lakhs of hectares are lost each fire season. Watchtowers see only fragments. Satellites report hours late. And in our cities, high-rise fires climb faster than ladders can reach. By the time smoke is visible from the ground, the window for early attack has already closed.

Detect in under 5 minutes

The first minutes decide everything. Abhai's AI identifies fire and smoke signatures the moment they appear — long before human eyes can.

Respond while it's still small

Enable initial attack within 10 minutes, while the fire is under one hectare — the difference between a routine callout and a week-long disaster.

Watch everything, always

Continuous 24×7 surveillance of vast forest divisions — through night, haze, and terrain no watchtower or patrol jeep can cover.

Proven, Not Promised

The intelligence inside Abhai is already flying.

This is not a lab experiment. The AI models at the heart of Abhai are field-deployed overseas, operating live with professional fire and emergency services in Europe — patrolling real forests, detecting real fires, and triggering real responses, season after season.

Abhai holds the exclusive rights to bring this proven technology home — re-engineered for Indian terrain, Indian weather, and Indian forest departments.

The Mission Loop

From patrol to suppression — humans only where it matters.

The entire loop is autonomous, with one deliberate exception: a human authorises the strike. Everything else happens on its own.

Plan

Missions are planned remotely from the command centre — coverage zones, patrol schedules, and risk priorities.

Patrol

Drones fly continuous autonomous sorties over forests and city skylines, scanning round the clock.

Detect & Predict

Onboard AI spots fire and smoke in real time and forecasts zones where ignition is most likely next.

Alert

Responders receive instant, geo-tagged alerts, and surveillance drones share exact coordinates with payload drones on standby.

Suppress

On human authorisation, suppression drones launch and attack the fire — reaching the zone before fire engines or crews arrive.

Beyond Detection

We don't just find the fire. We fight it.

Detection alone still leaves the fire burning until help arrives. Abhai closes that gap: our surveillance drones hand off exact coordinates to payload drones, and on a single human authorisation, the counter-attack is airborne — engaging the fire before the first fire engine or crew can reach the zone.
